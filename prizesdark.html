Dabble Roughie Rocket

The Dabble Roughie Rocket rewards whoever notches the Roughest Win of the competition — the single biggest points haul from one race.

You'll know who's in the running: a rocket icon appears next to their name on the Leaderboard, and their result is called out in the Roughest Win So Far banner.

If two or more tipsters are tied for the most points gained in a single race, the prize will be raffled between them to determine the winner.

Young Henrys Last Man Standing

The Young Henrys Last Man Standing rewards consistency throughout the Great Spring Tip Off.

To stay alive, simply score at least one (1) point every week. If you score zero (0) points in any week, you’ll be eliminated from the Last Man Standing competition.

The last entrant with an unbroken streak of scoring at least one point each week will be crowned the Young Henrys Last Man Standing and win the prize.
